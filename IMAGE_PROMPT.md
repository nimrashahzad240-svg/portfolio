References: the user’s 3D cartoon workshop screenshot (style and composition), their real portrait (likeness), the existing avatar (outfit and supporting likeness), and their red/pink/teal painting (artwork in the studio).

A landscape hero illustration for Nimra Shahzad’s personal portfolio: a recognizable young adult Pakistani woman with dark wavy hair, dark eyes and thick eyebrows, in a rust-brown jacket and cream clothing, assembling a freestanding tactile display of projecting ivory square blocks. Surround her with paintbrushes, a red/pink/teal canvas, blueprints, electronics, spare blocks, a screwdriver, calipers and a laptop. Warm afternoon light, honey wood, ivory, forest green, rust and blueprint blue. The block display represents Léiriú as an artwork, not a humanoid robot. Use the workshop reference for stylization, not its boy or robot character. No embedded lettering, logos or watermark.
